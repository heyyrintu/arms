# 01 · Product brief

## Product
ARMS Talent Partners: a premium workforce management website serving businesses **and** candidates as equal audiences. It combines corporate credibility with simple job discovery and two-way conversion journeys.

## Locked positioning
- Services: sourcing, recruitment, deployment, shift/attendance coordination, workforce administration and payroll support.
- Payroll employment arrangement: ARMS payroll or client payroll depending on the contract; clearly separate responsibilities.
- Brand voice: assured, practical, energetic, respectful, never patronizing or overpromising.
- Homepage title: **Great people. Greater possibilities.**
- Tagline: **Together for a Better Tomorrow.**
- Underlying creative idea: **The People Behind Progress** / People in Motion (internal language, not mandatory public slogan).
- Industry parity: Warehousing & Fulfillment, Transportation & Drivers, Cafés & Hospitality have equal weight.

## Audiences & jobs to be done
**Employer:** understand capability, validate cross-sector relevance, select workforce/payroll model, communicate requirements, request follow-up.
**Candidate:** discover verified roles quickly on mobile, understand expectations, apply without account, or leave a general talent registration; know where to seek support.
**ARMS staff:** publish/close jobs, review applications, organize candidates and employer enquiries, protect personal information, route enquiries appropriately.

## MVP scope
Public homepage, solutions detail, industry pages, about, contact/support, live approved jobs with filters and details, job applications, talent registration, employer requirements, secure administrative workflows, basic transactional acknowledgements, privacy and responsible deployment.

## Deliberate exclusions (MVP)
Employer self-serve portal, candidate accounts, individual attendance/payroll dashboard, time tracking hardware integration, automated screening/rejection, advanced matching algorithms, invoicing, payroll execution system, shift rostering engine, multilingual CMS unless confirmed necessary.

## Success measures (only after analytics/privacy approval)
- Qualified employer submissions received and actioned.
- Verified job views → completed eligible applications.
- Talent network registrations → recruiter-reviewed profiles.
- Form completion / abandonment and response-time operational targets (owner sets numeric targets).
- Accessibility, speed, uptime, and zero fabricated vacancies/claims.

## Critical open inputs
Verified contact details, legal business identity, service and employment scope, job feed, hosting, admin users, privacy and retention approvals, images/consents, authorized recruiter/support channels. See launch checklist.
