# 03 · Information architecture and page requirements

## Global header
Logo → home. Nav: **Solutions · Industries · Find Jobs · About · Contact**. Two persistent paths **Hire Workforce** and **Join Talent Network** (and clear Find Jobs menu). Desktop elegant compact navigation; mobile keyboard-friendly expandable menu. Support and privacy in footer.

| `/solutions` | Explain services | Discuss requirements | Three pillars; two payroll models; service clarity |

## Homepage editorial storyline
1. Promise: `Great people. Greater possibilities.` (65% typography-dominant hero)
2. Belief: `Businesses don't move themselves. People move them.`
3. Two equal audience journeys.
4. End-to-end workforce solutions: Build, Manage, Support.
5. Three equally prominent industries: warehouse, transport, hospitality.
6. Genuine latest jobs or truthful empty state + join network.
7. Six-step workforce lifecycle: Plan, Source, Deploy, Coordinate, Administer, Improve.
8. Closing: `Together for a Better Tomorrow` and equal CTAs.

## Navigation policy
Solutions answers **what** ARMS does. Industries answers **where**. Jobs answers **which verified roles are available**. Do not duplicate the same service copy verbatim across industry pages; link and contextualize.

## Content caveats
- Never silently replace unknown salary/contact/location with guesses.
- Do not show fictional demo jobs publicly.
- Provide candidate help routing tailored to the actual employing entity and contracted responsibilities.
