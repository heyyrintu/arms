# 08 · Photography production brief

## Agreed creative direction
**People at Work** + **Powerful & Ambitious**. The photograph conveys real work, skill, coordinated energy and operational scale. The site should feel like a premium editorial feature, not a staged recruitment advertisement.

## Hero shot
- One horizontal wide **operational panorama** with 3–5 people completing real tasks, one recognizable human focal point, supporting teamwork in middle ground, operational environment in background.
- Eye level/slightly low angle; realistic directional daylight; medium-high contrast; natural skin tones; neutral industrial environment; purposeful movement; authentic PPE and safe work practices.
- Subject can be from any of the three core industries, but must not falsely imply other industries are secondary. Other sectors receive equal prominence immediately in the Industries experience.
- Crop-safe for desktop wide panoramas, tablet and mobile; keep faces, actions and context visible, no headline over busy image.

## Equal industry photography shot list
1. Warehousing & Fulfillment: team scanning, receiving, picking, packing, loading, supervisor coordination.
2. Transportation & Drivers: licensed driver with vehicle at depot, fleet operations, dispatch coordination, safety checks.
3. Cafés & Hospitality: team working at service counter, kitchen operations, shift handoffs, customer-facing coordination.

Each industry must have comparable image dimensions, attention, lighting quality, professional depiction and visible space in the public design. Do not assign one a panoramic hero while others have tiny thumbnails.

## Look and feel
- Genuine moments. Natural expressions, workers engaged in tasks, respectful representation.
- Do not over-grade teal/orange, add fake sun rays, stage handshakes or create “perfect” environments.
- Preserve workplace reality, safety and human dignity. Avoid unsafe scenes or exposed records.

## Required image inventory for launch
`hero-wide`, `hero-mobile-crop`, `warehouse-index`, `transport-index`, `hospitality-index`, one per industry detail page (may be reused if composition supports it), `about-people` (approved genuine team/workplace imagery). Use optimized WebP/AVIF plus metadata/source/license records.

## Usage rights and accuracy
Get photographer/client/site permissions, individual consent where required, licensing terms and relevant alt text. No assumption that stock photo subjects are ARMS staff or that a warehouse belongs to an ARMS client. Existing `references/` files are **brand direction only**, not licensed real-site photos.
