# 04 · Working website copy deck

> Approved strategic direction, **not final legal/marketing sign-off**. The JSX pages already implement most text; use this file to preserve tone and priorities.

## Fixed brand language
- Brand: **ARMS Talent Partners**.
- Homepage headline: **Great people. Greater possibilities.**
- Tagline: **Together for a Better Tomorrow**.
- Hero eyebrow: **WORKFORCE MANAGEMENT & CAREER OPPORTUNITIES**.
- Hero support: **We help businesses build and manage the teams that keep operations moving, while connecting people with opportunities to build their future.**
- Equal CTAs: **Hire Workforce** / **Find Work**.

## Home chapters
- Manifesto: **Businesses don't move themselves. People move them.**
- Two journeys: **For the teams you build. For the future you pursue.**
- Solutions: **One connected workforce partner.**
- Industries: **Different worlds. One workforce partner.**
- Jobs: **Real work. Real opportunities.**
- Close: **People power progress. We help make the connection.**

## Service headings
- Build Your Workforce: **The right people for the work ahead.**
- Manage Your Workforce: **Make everyday operations work better.**
- Support Your Workforce: **Administration that supports people and business.**

## Industry headings, equal status
- Warehousing & Fulfillment: **The people behind every order.**
- Transportation & Drivers: **The people who keep things moving.**
- Cafés & Hospitality: **The people behind every experience.**

## Candidate messaging
- Job search: **Your next opportunity starts here.**
- Talent registration: **Bring your skills. Find possibilities.**
- Candidate support: **Support at every step.**
- Acknowledge applications but never promise placement or a response by a certain date without a real service-level commitment.

## Employer messaging
- Requirement enquiry: **Build a workforce ready for what's next.**
- Two employment/payroll models: **ARMS-managed** and **client-managed**, each defined by contract.
- Not every service is automatically included; display clear scope and limitations.

## Prohibited content unless verified
No company founding year, staff counts, deployment counts, claimed 99% accuracy, fake testimonials, named client logos, “100% compliant” legal guarantees, “guaranteed placement”, invented salaries, fixed availability claims, invented office address, email, phone or domain.
