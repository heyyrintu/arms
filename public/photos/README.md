# Workplace imagery

`concepts/` contains four original AI-generated workplace concepts requested by the owner on 14 November 2026. Every rendered image has a visible concept disclosure. These do not depict actual ARMS staff, clients or premises.

See `docs/16-GENERATED-IMAGERY.md` for provenance/usage limits and `docs/image-generation-prompts.json` for exact prompts. The images are optimized WebP; all industry sources share 1536 × 1024 dimensions and the same quality settings.

Genuine operational photos, permissions and final owner review remain launch requirements under `docs/08-PHOTOGRAPHY-BRIEF.md` and `docs/10-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`. No third-party stock image has been introduced.
